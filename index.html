About me

I care about the moment technology becomes understandable.

My work connects research, product strategy, UX/UI design, AI systems, and front-end collaboration. I like being the person who helps a team move from ambiguity to a product people can trust.

My design belief

I do not see AI as a visual effect or a shortcut. I see it as a product material that needs context, explanation, user control, and careful interaction design.

When I design, I ask: what should the user understand, decide, trust, question, and recover from?
